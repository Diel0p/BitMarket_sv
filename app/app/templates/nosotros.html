Meet BitMarket SV

Local commerce with Lightning payments, simple and real.

BitMarket connects buyers and sellers to power the local economy with Bitcoin Lightning: instant payments, low fees, and a clear experience for everyone.

Browse products I want to sell

Our mission

Give small and medium sellers a modern digital storefront, with Bitcoin Lightning payments that confirm in seconds and enable frictionless selling.

How it works

  1. Explore: the buyer finds products in the catalog.
  2. Pay: a Lightning invoice is generated for fast payment.
  3. Receive: the order is confirmed and the seller manages shipping.

Transparency and security

BitMarket keeps basic traceability for orders and payments so each operation has a clear status. Our goal is to make buying and selling trustworthy from the first click.
